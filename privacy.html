Privacy policy

This page explains what shahidkamal.dev and its assistant, SK-Mark I, do with information about visitors, and how the assistant uses Google user data.

Browsing the site

The site has no analytics, advertising or tracking scripts and sets no cookies of its own. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, may keep standard server logs such as IP addresses.

Chatting with SK-Mark I

  • Your questions and the assistant's answers are sent to a small server run on Cloudflare Workers, which passes them to an AI model (Anthropic's Claude, or an open model on Cloudflare Workers AI) to write the reply.
  • This site does not store or log the content of your questions. The conversation lives only in your browser tab and is gone when you close it.
  • Anthropic and Cloudflare process the messages under their own privacy policies.
  • To prevent abuse, the server applies rate limits based on your IP address.

Booking a meeting

  • If you book a meeting in the chat, you provide your name, your email address and, optionally, a topic.
  • These details are used only to create the meeting in Shahid's Google Calendar and to send you the calendar invitation with a Google Meet link. They are not sold, shared for marketing, or used for anything else.
  • To change or cancel a meeting, or to have your details deleted, email contact@shahidkamal.dev.

Google user data

  • The SK-Mark I app connects only to Shahid's own Google Calendar. It never asks visitors to sign in with Google and never accesses visitors' Google accounts.
  • It reads only when Shahid is busy or free, to offer open time slots, and it creates meeting events that visitors request. Visitors never see the titles or details of Shahid's other events.
  • SK-Mark I's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact@shahidkamal.dev.
